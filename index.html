Meet the founder

Thomas A. DeLoach

Thomas A. DeLoach is the founder of Clarity Compass 360, a coaching practice dedicated to helping high-achieving women gain clarity, build confidence, and move forward with purpose.

For more than 25 years, Thomas has served in preaching and teaching ministry, and for over 20 years he has served as a pastor, helping individuals navigate life's challenges, major transitions, personal growth, and important decisions. Throughout his ministry and leadership experience, he has guided people through seasons of uncertainty, career changes, relationship challenges, setbacks, and new opportunities.

Drawing on decades of experience listening, encouraging, mentoring, and helping people move forward, Thomas brings a thoughtful, practical, and supportive coaching approach. His passion is helping women overcome overthinking, gain confidence in their decision-making, discover renewed clarity, and take meaningful action toward the future they desire.

Thomas believes that lasting transformation begins with clarity, grows through confidence, and results in purposeful action.

About

Clarity for meaningful decisions. Confidence for what comes next.

Clarity Compass 360 helps high-achieving women who feel stuck, overwhelmed, uncertain, or at a crossroads in life gain the clarity and confidence needed to move forward.

Many women experience seasons when they question their next step, wrestle with important decisions, struggle with self-doubt, or feel successful on the outside yet unfulfilled on the inside. Clarity Compass 360 provides personalized coaching to help clients navigate those moments with greater confidence and direction.

Through thoughtful conversation, structured reflection, and practical action planning, we help women gain clarity about where they are, confidence in where they are going, and a roadmap for getting there.

Whether you're facing a career transition, seeking greater purpose, recovering from burnout, or simply trying to determine what's next, Clarity Compass 360 exists to help you move forward with clarity and confidence.
